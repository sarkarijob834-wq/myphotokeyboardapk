Camera or Gallery Photo as Keyboard Background

My Photo Keyboard lets you choose a gallery photo or take one with the camera. This guide covers which suits you and how to get a readable result. See also our full My Photo Keyboard guide.

Gallery or camera?

A gallery photo lets you choose a polished image. A camera shot is quick and personal.

Permissions

The app needs access to your photos or camera to do this. Grant only what you are comfortable with, and review it in Android settings.

Composition tips

Choose images with calm areas in the lower half, where the keys sit.

  • Check the current Play listing first.
  • Test in the apps you use most.

Brightness and contrast

Dark or muted photos usually keep white key text readable. Change font colour if the keyboard offers it.

Orientation

The listing says landscape and portrait backgrounds can be set separately, so prepare both.

Privacy of personal photos

A personal photo is stored on your device for use, but read the Data safety section, which lists photos and videos as data the app may collect.

FAQ

Do I need the camera permission?

Only if you want to take a photo inside the app.

Can I use different photos for orientations?

The listing says yes.

Will others see my photo?

Only your keyboard shows it on your screen.
