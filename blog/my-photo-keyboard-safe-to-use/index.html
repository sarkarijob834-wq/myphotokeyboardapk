Why keyboards need extra trust

Any keyboard can in principle see keystrokes. That is why you should read the permissions and privacy policy of every keyboard before using it for sensitive typing.

What the developer says

The Play description says the developer will never collect personal information. That is a statement by the developer, and we cannot independently confirm it.

What the Data safety section lists

The Data safety section says the app may collect photos and videos and device or other IDs, with no data shared with third parties and data encrypted in transit. It can change, so read the live version.

  • Check the current Play listing first.
  • Test in the apps you use most.

Ads you should expect

The listing is labelled Contains ads, and some reviews complain about ads. If ads bother you, compare alternatives first.

Practical precautions

Install only from Google Play, review permissions, and switch to a trusted keyboard for passwords, banking and one-time codes. Keep Android and Play Protect updated.

When to choose something else

If you want a keyboard without ads or with a company you already trust, compare options on Play. Our comparison table on the homepage is a starting point.
