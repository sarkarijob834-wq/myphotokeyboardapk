Set Photo as Keyboard Background on Android (Steps)

Want your own picture behind your keys? This guide shows how a photo keyboard app such as My Photo Keyboard handles it, based on the developer's Play Store description. See also our full My Photo Keyboard guide.

Why use a photo background

A photo keyboard makes typing feel personal. Many people choose a family picture, a pet or a calm landscape. It is cosmetic only, so it changes how the keyboard looks rather than how your phone works.

What you need first

You need an Android phone, an app that provides a keyboard with photo backgrounds, and a picture in your gallery or the ability to take one. Check your Android version against the app's Play Store page.

Install from Google Play

Install only from the official Play listing. Third-party APK sites cannot be vetted here, and Play gives you updates and Play Protect scanning.

  • Check the current Play listing first.
  • Test in the apps you use most.

Enable the keyboard

Open the app, tap the My Photo Keyboard button, then select it as your input method in Android's language and input settings. Menu names vary by phone brand.

Choose and preview your photo

Pick an image from the gallery or camera and look at the preview. Busy photos can make keys hard to read, so a simple or darker image often works better.

Apply a theme and test it

Add a theme if you like, then open a messaging app and type a few lines. If keys are hard to see, change the photo or the font colour setting.

FAQ

Does it work in every app?

The developer's description says it applies to the whole device and all apps once selected.

Can I use a camera photo?

Yes, the listing says you can use a gallery or camera photo.

Can I switch back?

Yes. Use the keyboard switcher or Android input settings to return to your usual keyboard.

Try it now

Follow the setup steps then get the app from Google Play.
