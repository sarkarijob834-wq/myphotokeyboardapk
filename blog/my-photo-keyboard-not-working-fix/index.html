My Photo Keyboard Not Working? Quick Fixes

If the keyboard will not appear or the photo will not change, work through these checks in order. See also our full My Photo Keyboard guide.

Keyboard not showing

Open Android language and input settings and make sure the keyboard is enabled, then choose it from the keyboard switcher.

Photo not applying

Re-select the photo, tap apply and reopen the app you are typing in.

Ads blocking the app

Reviews mention ads that interrupt use. Closing and reopening the app may help, but if it persists consider another keyboard.

  • Check the current Play listing first.
  • Test in the apps you use most.

Slow or laggy typing

Restart your phone, free storage and check for an app update in Google Play.

Reinstall

Uninstall, reinstall from Google Play and set it up again. Your chosen photo may need to be reselected.

Still stuck

Contact the developer through the support details on the Play listing, or switch to a different keyboard.

FAQ

Why does it keep switching back?

Some phones reset input methods after updates. Re-select it.

Is the problem my phone?

Possibly. Test other keyboards.

Where do I report bugs?

Use the Support section on the Play listing.

Try it now

Follow the setup steps then get the app from Google Play.
