What is My Photo Keyboard?

Quick answer: My Photo Keyboard is an ad-supported Android keyboard app that sets a photo from your gallery or camera as the keyboard background across the whole device and every app. Google Play lists the developer as Abbott Cullen and the category as Photography.

It sits in the same family as other custom keyboards: you install it, switch your input method to it, and then it replaces your default keyboard until you switch back. Beyond appearance it offers typing tools such as spell check, next-word prediction and swipe typing. See the features or jump to the setup steps.

The listing says more than 50 languages are supported. Confirm yours in the app.

Keyboard height, font colour, key sound and vibration, plus separate landscape and portrait backgrounds.

How to set up My Photo Keyboard

  1. Install from Google Play.
  2. Open the app and tap the My Photo Keyboard button.
  3. Select it as your input method in Android settings.
  4. Choose a gallery or camera photo and check the preview.
  5. Pick a theme and test it in a chat app.

These steps follow the developer's description. Menu names differ by phone brand. For a longer walk-through read how to set a photo as keyboard background.

Why people use a photo keyboard

Most people want their phone to feel like theirs. A keyboard is the part of the screen you see most, so a family photo, a pet or a favourite place turns routine typing into something personal. Others like the extras bundled in: themes, fonts and emoji art for livelier chats, or separate landscape and portrait looks. It is mainly a style choice, so judge it by how comfortable it is to type with, not only how it looks.

Types of keyboard customisation

  • Photo backgrounds: your own image behind the keys, the core idea here.
  • Colour themes: preset colours and key styles with no personal photo.
  • Font styles: decorative lettering. It may not display correctly in every app.
  • Layout settings: height, key sounds, vibration and tablet layouts.

Tips for a readable keyboard

  • Choose darker or muted photos with white key text, or adjust font colour.
  • Keep busy detail away from the lower half, where the keys sit.
  • Prepare separate landscape and portrait images.
  • Test in the apps you use most before settling.
  • Switch to a trusted keyboard for passwords and banking.

Privacy, ads and safety

A keyboard can in principle see what you type, so trust matters. The Play description says the developer never collects personal information, while the Data safety section says the app may collect photos and videos and device or other IDs, shares no data with third parties and encrypts data in transit. These are the developer's statements and we cannot verify them.

  • Read the developer privacy policy.
  • Expect ads; reviews mention them.
  • Use a trusted keyboard for sensitive typing.

Troubleshooting

  • Keyboard missing: enable it in Android language and input settings, then pick it in the switcher.
  • Photo not changing: reselect it and apply again.
  • Laggy: restart, free storage and update from Google Play.
  • Ads in the way: try another keyboard.

Frequently asked questions

Is My Photo Keyboard free?

The Play Store listing shows the app as installable, with ads (it is labelled "Contains ads"). Check the current listing for any in-app purchases or changes.

Who made My Photo Keyboard?

The Play Store lists the developer as Abbott Cullen. This website is an independent guide and is not run by them.

Can I use my own camera or gallery photo?

Yes. The listing says you can set a background from your gallery or take one with the camera, with a preview before you apply it.

Does it work in all apps?

The listing says it applies to the whole device and all apps, once it is selected as your input method.

How many languages does it support?

The listing says 50+ languages. Availability of specific layouts may differ, so check inside the app.

Are there themes, fonts and emoji?

The listing mentions multiple themes, 50+ font styles, 500+ emoji, emoji art and GIF stickers.

Does it show ads?

Yes, it is labelled as containing ads, and some user reviews complain about ad placement. Consider this before installing.

What data may it collect?

The Data safety section says it may collect photos and videos and device or other IDs, and that no data is shared with third parties. Read the current section yourself, as developers can update it.

How do I switch to it as my keyboard?

Open the app, tap the My Photo Keyboard button, then choose it as your input method in Android settings, as described in the listing.

Is it rated for children?

The listing shows a 3+ content rating. That is a content rating, not a privacy assessment.

Does it support swipe typing and voice typing?

The listing mentions swipe typing, speak to type, next-word prediction and auto spell check.

Should I download it from here?

No. This site does not host any APK files. Install only from Google Play to get official updates and Play Protect scanning.
